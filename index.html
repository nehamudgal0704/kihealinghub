A small pause

Let your breath set the pace.

Settle into a comfortable position and follow the gentle rhythm. You can stop or return to your natural breathing at any time.

A simple, even rhythm: breathe in for five seconds, then out for five. Keep your breath easy and unforced.

This pacing guide is for relaxation, not medical care. Skip breath holds if they feel uncomfortable, and return to normal breathing whenever you need.
