The Principles of Ki

Understanding your vital energy

In Japanese traditions, Ki is understood as vital energy, with related concepts found in Chinese Qi and Sanskrit Prana. These practices offer ways to focus attention, movement, and breath.

Center

The Hara / Tanden

Often described as a center below the navel, the tanden is a focus in some Japanese movement and meditation practices. Gentle attention here can support a grounded practice.

Tradition

Meridian Flow

Traditional systems describe pathways through which Ki flows. We approach these ideas as part of a cultural wellness practice, alongside comfortable movement and reflection.

Practice

Reiki Integration

Reiki is a complementary practice rooted in Japanese tradition. Sessions are offered for relaxation and reflection, not as a substitute for medical or mental health care.
